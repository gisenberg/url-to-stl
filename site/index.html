THE COMPLETE WORKFLOW

Design, slice, scan.

The generator validates the QR, builds one watertight token, and writes Bambu’s native layer event into the project. You still choose the physical AMS slots in the print dialog.

  1. 01

    Enter a URL

    Choose the diameter, layer heights, relief, colors, and project filaments.

  2. 02

    Download the 3MF

    The filament change is saved before the first layer containing QR geometry.

  3. 03

    Slice and print

    Open it as a project, map the AMS slots, inspect the preview, and scan the first print.

BUILT FOR THE REAL PRINT

See the swap before you slice.

The local workspace previews the token, verifies the encoded URL, automatically increases the diameter when QR modules would be too small for the nozzle, and shows the exact change layer and Z height.

  • Four-module QR quiet zone
  • Automatic printable-diameter clamp
  • Real-scale X2D print-bed preview
  • Contrast and nozzle-size safeguards
  • STL and PNG exports included
  • Custom Bambu project profiles supported
